The Problem

AI shopping agents are reshaping commerce, but brands have no way to optimize for discovery

Invisible to AI

Products optimized for keyword search are invisible to LLM reasoning. Traditional SEO doesn't work when agents understand intent, not keywords.

No Visibility

Brands have no insight into how AI agents perceive their products. Why does a product rank #5 instead of #1? What's missing?

No Validation

Lab metrics don't predict production outcomes. Optimizations that test well may fail in real traffic, wasting resources.

Protocol Gap

Commerce protocols (UCP, ACP) define transactions, but not discovery. The layer between agent reasoning and checkout is undefined.
